Selected media coverage of my research.

**Cash or Card? Impression Management and Restaurant Tipping Behavior** (with King King Li), *Journal of Behavioral and Experimental Economics*, 2022 — ranked in the top 5% of all research outputs tracked by Altmetric.

Using data from several hundred transactions at a large Hong Kong restaurant, the paper finds that diners are markedly less likely to tip — and tip less generously — when paying by card rather than cash. The results suggest that tipping is partly an exercise in impression management: handing over cash is a visible act of generosity, while a card payment conceals it. As the pandemic accelerated the shift to cashless payment, the findings spoke directly to restaurateurs' fears about vanishing tips, prompting coverage in the Swiss press.
